HybridTumorNet: 3D Tumor Infiltration Solver (PINNs + CNNs)

A novel 3-head deep learning architecture designed to model glioblastoma tumor growth by coupling Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) with 3D CNNs. Built on a MONAI 3D ResNet-50 backbone pre-trained on medical datasets.

  • Multi-Parametric MRI Inputs: Processes T1, T1ce, T2, and FLAIR modalities from the BraTS 2021 dataset as a unified 4-channel 3D volume.
  • Three Independent Decoder Heads:
    1. Density Decoder: Predicts continuous tumor cell density field u(x,t).
    2. Physics Decoder: Infers spatio-variant biophysical parameters (Diffusion Coefficient D(x) & Proliferation Rate ρ(x)).
    3. Segmentation Decoder: Outputs multi-class softmax segmentation for tumor sub-regions.
  • Governing Physics: Integrates the Fisher-KPP reaction-diffusion equation directly into the multi-task loss function to enforce biological constraints.
  • Clinical Application: Capable of forecasting tumor density at 30+ days via PDE simulation, visualized using ITK-SNAP.
